# Soccer feature test — 2026-09-17

**Question:** the soccer score is half price and half xG. Would anything else help: home/away, team attack,
opponent defence, the team's expected goals, minutes, penalty duty, player form, team form?

**Data:** 1,126 legs the board could bet (confirmed starters priced +100 or longer) across 18 graded nights
(08-27 to 09-15); 330 of them scored. Team ratings come from understat and use only matches played before
each night: last season counts as 8 games, then this season's matches are added. Player stats come from
that night's `xg.psv`.

**Method:** each feature is added on top of the price in a logistic model. It is fit on earlier nights,
then scored on the next night (12 nights scored). The 90% bands come from resampling whole nights.

## Does it pick better bets? (walk-forward, top 8 a night, flat 1u, 96 bets)

| ranking | scored | ROI | 90% band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **live TOTAL (today)** | 44 (46%) | **+17.0%** | [−4%, +39%] |
| price only | 43 (45%) | +8.7% | [−7%, +24%] |
| price + xG, as EV | 30 (31%) | +8.9% | [−18%, +40%] |

## Verdict

- **Nothing new beats the price.** Home, minutes, penalties and form add nothing. The team-level terms
  (attack, defence, expected goals, form) make the held-out predictions *worse*. The betting price already
  includes the team's strength and the matchup, and four or five games into a season a team rating is
  mostly noise.
- **The xG score is the only extra input with a faint positive signal** (z 1.8), and the live TOTAL is
  still the best ranking tested. **No change to the live model.**
- **Main caveat: sample size.** 18 nights is small. Re-run as nights accumulate:
  `python3 build_rows.py && python3 test_features.py`. `teams_understat.psv` must be refreshed first
  (understat `getLeagueData/<league>/<season>`).
- **Side note:** confirmed starters at +100 or longer scored 29.3% against 27.2% implied by the prices
  held at build. So the posted lineup, not the stats, is where the board's edge comes from.
