# Layered scorer model — first results (2026-09-17)

**Question:** is there a layer of data that predicts who scores better than xG alone, and better than the price?

**Data:** 12.7 seasons of understat data (2014/15 to 2026/27 so far) for the five top leagues: 648,761 player
appearances by 9,883 players, and 43,578 team-match rows. It is on the `soccer-data` branch under
`history/2014-2026-limit0/`. The football-data.co.uk closing prices could not be downloaded (the host
refuses GitHub runners), so the market version of the team layer is not built yet.

## The algorithm

```
lambda = team goals expected tonight  x  his share of team xG  x  expected minutes / 90
       + team penalty xG               x  his penalty share      x  expected minutes / 90
P(scores) = 1 - exp(-lambda), then a small calibration step fitted on earlier seasons only
```

- **Team goals expected tonight** = league average × the team's attack rating × the opponent's
  defence rating × home or away. The ratings are exponentially weighted, carry over (reduced) from
  last season, and use only matches before that day.
- **His share** = his non-penalty xG divided by the team's non-penalty xG while he was on the
  pitch. It is weighted toward recent matches and pulled toward an average for his position.

## 1. Historical walk-forward: 2017–2026, 346k starts, each season scored by a model trained only on earlier seasons

| model | AUC | AUC among the likeliest 40% | top 3 per matchday scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| share × minutes only (no team layer) | 0.754 | 0.651 | 33.2% |
| **full layered model** | **0.763** | **0.663** | **36.3%** |
| without the penalty layer | 0.760 | 0.663 | 35.8% |
| without the minutes layer | 0.762 | 0.662 | 35.9% |
| + form, hot hand, shot volume, involvement, team form, rest | 0.763–0.765 | 0.662–0.664 | 36.2–36.6% |
| every input, gradient boosting | 0.764 | 0.662 | 36.6% |

- **The team layer does the most work:** the top-3 hit rate goes from 33% to 36%.
- **Penalties help a little.**
- **Everything else adds almost nothing** once the layers are in, and a nonlinear model is no better.
  So the signal is in the structure (team × share × minutes), not in extra stats.
- **Calibration** is within about 1 point across all deciles.

## 2. On the board's own bets

Of the 1,165 bettable legs (confirmed starters priced −200 or longer), 791 league legs over 15 nights
could be matched to understat. Cup, European and MLS legs are not matched yet.

| ranking | AUC | top 8 a night scored | ROI, flat 1u | 90% band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| price | 0.648 | 44% | +6.8% | [−9%, +23%] |
| live TOTAL | 0.644 | 48% | +23.8% | [+2%, +46%] |
| **layered model** | **0.662** | 46% | +21.4% | [+2%, +42%] |
| model + price, averaged | **0.663** | 48% | +20.4% | [+1%, +41%] |

- **Independent information test:** a logistic fit of the result on logit(price) and log(model λ)
  gives a model coefficient of **+0.79 (p = 0.002)** and a price coefficient of +0.52 (p = 0.055).
  Adding the current edge_z on top adds nothing (p = 0.15). **So the layered model carries
  information the price does not, and it replaces the xG half of TOTAL.**
- **Sample caveat:** 15 nights. The ROI bands overlap, and the walk-forward on the last 9 nights ties
  with TOTAL.
