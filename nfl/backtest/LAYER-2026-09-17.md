# NFL layered scorer model and the blend (NFLLAYER-2026-09-17)

The owner asked: *"can you run the same algorithm you did with soccer on football?"* After seeing the results: *"the two combined clearly seems to be the way to go"*. It shipped live the same day, before DET@BUF. The code is in `nfl/nfl_layer.py`, which `nfl_mock.score()` calls.

## The model

```
lambda = team TDs expected       (a + b * implied team total)
       x his touch share         (EW carries+targets share, half-life 8 games, K=1 to half the position mean)
       x his xTD per touch       (EW, half-life 17, K=60 touches to the position mean) / league mean
p_model = sigmoid(0.870 + 0.463 * logit(p_live) + 0.975 * log(lambda))
```

- **xTD:** P(TD) for a carry or target, by yards to goal. It was fitted on 2016–18 only.
- **Decay clock:** the off-season counts as 6 weeks, so last season carries into the current one.

The first version was a straight port of soccer: one exponentially weighted xTD share. It ranked worse than the live model (AUC .739 vs .757). Splitting that share into **volume × touch quality**, with a long, heavily shrunk quality term, closed the gap.

## Walk-forward results

The population is the live backtest's own: `weeks_2021..2025.pkl`, played rows only, 37,675 rows. `nfl_model.json` was fitted on 2021–24, so 2025 is the only clean season for the live model.

| season | AUC layer | AUC live | log loss layer | log loss live |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | .747 | .749 | .372 | .381 |
| 2022 | .745 | .741 | .356 | .370 |
| 2023 | .761 | .757 | .350 | .366 |
| 2024 | .767 | .768 | .361 | .374 |
| 2025 | .763 | .766 | .360 | .374 |

- **Top 8 per week that scored:** layer 56.9%, live 55.7%.
- **Inside the likeliest 40%:** the layer out-ranks the live model in all five seasons. In 2025 it was .662 vs .646.
- **Independent information:** in logistic regressions of y on logit(p_live) + log(lambda), both terms have p < .001 in every season, and in 2025 alone.
- **Blend fitted on 2022–24 and tested on 2025:** AUC .771, log loss .356. The live model alone scored .766 / .374, the layer alone .763 / .360.

## Real prices, 2026 week 1

The sample is 4 slates, 219 priced and matched players, 53 scorers.

- **AUC:** layer .741, de-vigged price .736, board TOTAL .508.
- **Top 8 in the +100..+400 band:** price 8/29, layer 6/29. Every ranking lost money. The sample is far too small to read.

## Not shown
