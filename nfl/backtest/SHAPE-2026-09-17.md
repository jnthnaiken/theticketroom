# Should football copy the soccer board? (NFLSHAPE-2026-09-17)

Owner: *"feel like the same set up as soccer is the way to go"* → *"why not use the algorithm to find out. thats what it was for."* So it was measured instead of argued.

Soccer's shape is **Top Bin**: the 8 strongest confirmed starters as 1u singles, no moons, plus one lunch and one nightcap. Football's shape is **4 anchors → 8 moons (3-leg round robins) + anchor singles**, everything capped at +400.

## The run

`run.py` gained three rules alongside the live one, all at the +400 cap, all on the blended model that went live today (NFLLAYER):

| rule | draft |
|---|---|
| `live_cap400` | 4 anchors, 8 moons, singles (LIVE) |
| `topbin6/8/12_cap400` | `ANCH=0`, `TOP_SINGLES=6/8/12` — the soccer draft, football prices |

2024 + 2025, 115 slates, four synthetic books each (see `RESULTS-2026-09-15.md` for why a book here is a model of the market). The realistic read is `same` + `rich` + `sharp`; `weak` is the world where we know more than the book, and it flatters everything.

## Result — the moons stay

| rule | ROI (same+rich+sharp) | 90% band | u/slate | stake/slate | worst slate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **live: 4 anchors + 8 moons** | **+15.3%** | +1 .. +30 | +1.33u | 8.7u | −22.0u |
| top 6 singles | +4.0% | −2 .. +10 | +0.21u | 5.2u | −9.0u |
| top 8 singles | +2.3% | −4 .. +9 | +0.14u | 5.8u | −11.0u |
| top 12 singles | +1.3% | −4 .. +7 | +0.09u | 7.0u | −12.3u |

- **The moons are what earns.** Split by ticket type under the live rule: moons +23.8%, singles +4.0%. Singles drafted the soccer way return +4.3% — the same as the singles football already drafts. **Copying soccer would drop the moons and keep the half that barely pays.**
- **True in every book world.** Live beats top-8 under `same` (+36% vs +15%), `rich` (+39% vs +11%) and `weak` (+132% vs +45%), and loses by less under `sharp` only because it stakes more (−30% vs −20%).
- **Why the answer differs from soccer.** A 3-leg football moon inside +100..+400 is a much shorter shot than a soccer round robin was: the moons hit 20.8% a leg-set against 29.0% for singles, and the round-robin structure pays enough at those prices. In soccer the same structure needed two of four long legs and lost.
- **The cost is variance.** The live shape stakes 8.7u a slate against 5.8u, and its worst slate is −22u against −11u. 57% of slates lose either way; the moons pay for it in the tail (best slate +104u).

## Caveats, and they are not small

1. **The blend's weights were fitted on 2022–25, so both backtest seasons are partly in-sample.** The ROI levels here are optimistic. The *comparison between shapes* is fair — both run the same model — which is what the question was.
2. **2024 loses for every shape** (live −7.0%, top-8 −4.8%); 2025 carries the result (live +37.9%).
3. **One graded football week of real prices exists.** Everything above is simulated books.

## Decision

No change. Football keeps 4 anchors and 8 moons at the +400 cap; soccer keeps Top Bin. Re-run after ~6 graded weeks of real prices:

    cd nfl/backtest && BT_RULES=live_cap400,topbin8_cap400 ./run_all.sh
