Using the board

  1. Start on Tickets — tonight’s board, already drafted. Read it top to bottom: 🍱 Early Window, 🌃 Sunday Night, ⚓️ Anchors, 🏈 Paydirt. The card at the top is the running season ledger. The View chips filter to one kind at a time.
  2. Check the lock line before you act on anything. A slip marked 🔒 is frozen and is what the ledger grades. Anything still projected can change as lineups post — the board re-fetches itself every few minutes and adopts the newer draft.
  3. Open Players to see the field the board drafted from. Every player who cleared the pool gate, one card each. Sort by model score, shortest price, confirmed first, team or name, jump straight to a player, or search. The four chalk favourites show up here but are barred from every ticket, so you will not find them on a slip.
  4. Use Builder to build and grade a slip of your own. Search or filter by game, tick the bats you like, then hit 🔨 Build. It comes back as a kitchen inspection: a letter grade, a build score out of 100 on an F-to-A meter, and a curve showing where your slip lands against comparable slips drawn from tonight’s live bats — same shape, same price range — with your marker sitting somewhere between weaker and stronger.
  5. Read the notes, then take the grade for what it is. Inspector’s notes say in plain English what works about the slip and what doesn’t, and the line-up panel breaks out every bat with its price, a conviction bar and its own chance of going deep. In the board’s own words: a high grade means a well-built slip, not an edge on the book.
