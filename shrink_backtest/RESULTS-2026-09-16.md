# Batted-ball shrinkage vs the 80 floor — backtest, 2026-09-16

**Question (owner):** instead of a hard batted-ball floor (`MIN_CARD_BIP`, raised 40 → 80 today by
`CARDBIP80-2026-09-16`), pull each bat's card rates toward the league mean in proportion to his sample:
`rate' = (n·rate + k·mean) / (n + k)`, n = Kasper BIP. Ship only if it beats the floor.

**Verdict: NOT SHIPPED.** It does not beat the 80 floor. The floor stays.

## Method
- `calibration.jsonl`, the 59 nights that carry `k_bip` (13,835 priced bats).
- Replays the live pipeline: kas_v1 (`kas_weights_v1.json`, `kasmodel.score_rows`) → EV (`ev_weights_v1.json`)
  → rank by EV. The no-shrink replay tracks the logged `kas_v1` at r = 0.9975.
- Shrunk: `c_hh`, `c_la` (raw LA shrunk, then the bell), `c_fb`, `c_dmg` (ISO / xwOBAcon). Priors = means of
  bats with ≥ 300 BIP: HH 47.3, LA 17.8, FB 27.0, dmg 0.469.
- "live" = floor 80 with bats under 80 excluded from the ranking (THINBAT).

## Results
Ranking quality over every priced bat, mean per-night AUC (HR yes/no):

| variant | kas_v1 AUC | EV AUC |
|---|---|---|
| **live: floor 80 + exclude** | **0.6081** | **0.5882** |
| shrink k=50 | 0.6062 | 0.5874 |
| shrink k=100 | 0.6054 | 0.5871 |
| shrink k=150 | 0.6049 | 0.5873 |
| shrink k=250 | 0.6034 | 0.5865 |
| shrink k=150 + exclude < 80 | 0.6064 | 0.5881 |

Top-30 per night, HR-rate difference vs live (paired night bootstrap, 90%):

| variant | diff |
|---|---|
| shrink k=100 | +0.17 pp [−0.56, +0.90] |
| shrink k=150 | +0.62 pp [−0.17, +1.41] |
| shrink k=250 | +0.68 pp [−0.23, +1.58] |
| shrink k=150 + exclude < 80 | +0.51 pp [−0.17, +1.19] |

Top-22 / top-30 flat ROI moved by a few points in both directions depending on k and N (the best k flips
between the two cuts) — noise, not signal.

## Reading
- On ranking the whole field the floor is marginally better than every shrink setting.
- At the top of the board shrinkage is +0.5–0.7 pp, with every interval spanning zero.
- The EV layer puts little weight on the kas score (`b_kz` = 0.093), so card-rate changes move p only
  slightly; the floor works by removing the bats outright.
- kas_v1 was fit on unshrunk season stats. A fair test of shrinkage needs a refit with shrunk inputs in the
  Statcast training table — that is the next step if this is revisited, not a live patch.
